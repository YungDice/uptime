Scope: the Uptime app shell - Home, People, Boards. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: people who already keep streaks (WHOOP, Duolingo, Strava, Snapchat)
and will judge this against those apps. Job: confirm a number is still running,
in about four seconds, on a screen they open once a month.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Uptime is the iPhone stopwatch that was never stopped - the artifact
the whole trend is made of - so the app IS that screen, not a fitness
dashboard quoting it. Refuses the card-stack streak dashboard and its flame.

OWN-WORLD: iOS Clock grammar. True-black ground, white numerals at light
weight with tabular figures, hairline separators at 0.5px, no cards and no
shadows. The Timer ring with its tick track carries the check-in window.
Two pill buttons, tinted-fill on dark. Orange owns the live run, green owns
banked time, red owns a lapse, grey owns everything else.

STORY: the clock is real, it is still running, and the time on it is mine to
give away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: black field, the ring centred and near-full-bleed; inside it
the day count large and the running HH:MM:SS.ss beneath, hundredths moving;
two pill buttons under the ring; lap-style ruled rows below the fold.

FORM: brief-pinned by the user ("it should look like iphone timer ui, it
should have miniseconds"), superseding assigned index 6 (Tide Table) from
seed key 75c28eff.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

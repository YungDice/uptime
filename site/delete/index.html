Delete your Uptime account

For Uptime: Streak Stopwatch, by Yung Dice.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Email mgmt.yungdice@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Uptime account and include your nickname.
  3. We reply to confirm, and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password, nickname
  • Your profile picture
  • Your stopwatch, streaks and history
  • Who you follow, who follows you, and gifts you sent or received
  • Invite codes and push-notification tokens

What is kept

  • Purchase records, kept by the payment provider and us for as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically up to 7 years). They are no longer linked to a profile.
  • Server backups, which roll over and are gone within 30 days of deletion.
